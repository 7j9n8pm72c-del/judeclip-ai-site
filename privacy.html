Privacy Policy

Draft dated 12 November 2026.

Pre-release legal draft.

The responsible operator's verified legal name, private contact address and any required German legal notice must be added before this site is submitted for TikTok production app review. Do not post private information in public GitHub issues.

1. Responsible operator

JudeClip AI is an independent desktop software project. The verified controller identity and private privacy-request contact are pending. General project questions are accepted through public GitHub issues, but GitHub issues are public and are not a suitable channel for private data requests.

2. Website hosting

This informational website is hosted using GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. The published HTML does not use analytics scripts, advertising pixels or account registration. GitHub may collect network information, including IP addresses and request logs, to provide and secure the website. Read GitHub's privacy statement for its practices.

3. Local video processing

The desktop app uses local software for video rendering, speech transcription, clips, captions and reports. Files stay on your computer until you delete them. You are responsible for the video files you select and how you protect the computer.

4. Video imports

When you provide an online video link, the app connects to the relevant source platform to retrieve authorized footage. The service may receive network information. The local transcription model might also require a first-time download from its provider.

5. TikTok access

If you choose to connect your TikTok account, TikTok's authorization service returns access credentials to your local app. The app uses authorized credentials to send an individually approved clip to TikTok's inbox and check its upload status. TikTok receives the video when you approve the transfer. Review TikTok's privacy policy.

6. Captions and publication

JudeClip AI prepares caption and hashtag suggestions locally. The TikTok inbox upload does not publish the video or attach the text automatically. You complete the post in TikTok.

7. Retention and revocation

Delete your local outputs when no longer needed. Disconnect TikTok within the app and revoke app access inside TikTok account settings to stop future access. Information already transferred to TikTok is governed by its own settings and retention rules.

8. GDPR and contact

Where data protection law applies, rights to access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and supervisory-authority complaint may apply. The operator must publish the appropriate lawful bases, retention details, contact method and any legally required notice before treating this draft as final.

9. Revisions

This draft will be updated when operator details and the final data-processing configuration are confirmed.
